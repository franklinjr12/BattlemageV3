# First-time playtest sheet

Use the Windows release, not F1/debug tools. Do not explain controls to the tester. Record the version, package, difficulty, seed, and total elapsed time.

1. Can the player create a character and identify the next action in the hub?
2. How long until they move with right-click, select with QWER, confirm with left-click, and dodge?
3. Can they tell an unavailable spell from an invalid target? Do they understand rooted casts and dodge cancellation?
4. Do telegraphs explain hits and misses? Can they distinguish projectile, ground, cone, and heavy attacks?
5. Do they discover Burn/Combustion, Freeze/Shatter, or displacement/area combinations?
6. Do purchases and level choices change their decisions? Can they equip and compare all four gear slots?
7. Does the faster enemy force a defensive response? Does the Oracle attract priority? Are mage duels different from packs?
8. Does Champion phase two feel fair? Is defeat/retry understandable?
9. Close/reopen from the hub; verify the build, gold, XP, offers, and progression resume correctly.
10. Complete the demo and start another character. Verify no old progression leaks and settings remain.

Record confusion as observed behavior before proposing features. Prefer wording, timing, and feedback corrections. Gather playtime across at least three unfamiliar players before accepting the 30–60 minute target. Test all three packages and at least one pure-school build. Listen on headphones and speakers for masking, fatigue, startling transients, and missing cues.

Release gates: no progression blocker, no stuck input/cast state, correct save/rewards, understandable core controls, multiple enjoyable builds, and confirmed first-run duration. Do not mark these passed using automated bot results alone.
